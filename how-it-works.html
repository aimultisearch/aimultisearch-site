Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions about AI Multi Search.

What is AI Multi Search?
⌄

AI Multi Search lets you ask one question and receive answers from multiple AI providers at the same time, so you can compare responses easily.

Which AI providers are available?
⌄

The available AI providers depend on your plan and settings. You can enable or disable providers anytime in Settings → AIs displayed.

What plans are available?
⌄

AI Multi Search offers three plans:

  • Basic (free)
  • Pro
  • Ultra

Available features, AI providers, and usage limits depend on your plan.

Is AI Multi Search free?
⌄

Yes. A Basic (free) plan is available. Pro and Ultra unlock additional features, providers, or higher limits.

How do subscriptions work?
⌄

All subscriptions are managed securely by the store you downloaded the app from — Google Play on Android, the App Store on iPhone and iPad. Payments, renewals, and cancellations are handled directly through your Google or Apple account.

Can I cancel my subscription?
⌄

Yes. You can cancel anytime from Google Play (Android) or from your App Store subscription settings (iPhone and iPad). Your plan remains active until the end of the current billing period.

What is the Insight function?
⌄

The Insight function helps you turn multiple AI answers into clearer takeaways.

It analyzes responses from different AI providers and generates structured results such as Summary • Analysis • Conclusion • Common Points • Differences • Insight.

Can I search with a photo?
⌄

Yes. Camera Search lets you take a photo or pick an image from your gallery, then send it to several AI providers at once and compare how each one analyzes it.

Tap the camera icon in the search bar, choose your image, add a question if you want one — and select at least two AIs. Adding text is optional: if you leave it empty, the app asks the AIs to describe and analyze the image.

Camera Search is available on the Pro and Ultra plans. Pro includes a selection of vision-capable AIs; Ultra unlocks all of them.

Does Camera Search use my normal search limit?
⌄

No. Camera Search has its own separate daily allowance, so using it never reduces your regular text searches.

One image counts as one use, no matter how many AI providers you compare it with. The allowance is only used once your request is actually sent — taking a photo costs nothing.

Can I use Insight on a photo search?
⌄

Yes. Insight works the same way on image results as on text results: select the answers you trust and generate a Summary • Analysis • Conclusion from them. Available Insight modes still depend on your plan.

Does the app store my questions?
⌄

AI Multi Search only forwards your request to the selected AI providers. Some providers may process requests according to their own privacy policies.

What happens to the photos I send?
⌄

Your image is resized and compressed on your device, then forwarded to the AI providers you selected in order to answer your request. AI Multi Search does not store your photos — the image is used to process your request and nothing more. It is not saved on our servers, not published, not shared with anyone else, and the app does not build a photo library from it.

Saving a result keeps the answers only — the photo itself is never kept.

As with text, each AI provider handles the content it receives according to its own privacy policy.

Are my saved results private?
⌄

Yes. Saved results are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded or shared.

Why do different AIs give different answers?
⌄

Each AI provider has its own training data, style, and reasoning methods. Differences are normal and are exactly what AI Multi Search is designed to show.

What are credits?
⌄

Some AI providers may use credits depending on usage.

AI Multi Search allows all users on the Basic (free) plan to try certain premium AI providers every day, so you can explore advanced models before upgrading.

Can I choose how answers are displayed?
⌄

Yes. You can select different result layouts such as vertical list, carousel, or grid.

Change it anytime in Settings → Results layout.

How can I report a bug or send feedback?
⌄

Go to Settings → Support & feedback. Your message will be sent by email with a clear subject for faster handling.

Who is AI Multi Search for?
⌄

Anyone who wants to compare AI answers, double-check information, or explore different explanations and writing styles.
